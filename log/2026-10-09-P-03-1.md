## Done
- `card plan` runs the checks Dependencies, Specification and Coverage after Sizes, in the order of the table in design section 11.1.
- Dependencies: a dependency that is not an id, or names neither a row nor a card; a cycle through rows and cards together, found with the loop of `lint_cards`.
- Specification: a `spec` file that is missing, untracked, unreadable, or not a regular file.
- Coverage: a `#` heading at the outline's level, outside a code fence, that no row cites and that is not under `Not planned`; a row that is not done, or the `Not planned` list, citing a heading the specification lacks at that level. Done is read from the base branch.
- A specification that differs from `spec_blob` is one line on stdout, and the exit status stays 0.
- `mk_outline` in test/lib.sh writes and stages the specification, and records its hash as `spec_blob`.

## Deviations
- An outline with a format finding is not checked for headings that no row cites: a row or an item the reader dropped would show as false findings, and tests of P-02b count findings. The reviewer noted that CI can go red twice for such an outline.
- `test_plan_passes_an_outline_with_no_not_planned_section`, of P-02, now cites every heading; the new rule fails it otherwise.
- A specification that is a link, not a regular file, or unreadable fails, each with its own message. The card names only missing and untracked.
- Headings are compared as the tests gate compares names, with two additions from the review: a byte above 127 is kept, and a heading with no letter or digit is compared as written. Without them a heading in a non-Latin script was never required to be cited.
- A fence may be longer than three marks, and ends at a line of the same mark, at least as long, with nothing after it. The card says only "three backticks or three tildes at the start of a line".
- Whether a dependency exists is read from the cards of the working tree and of the base branch; the edges of the cycle check come from the working tree's cards only. Of an id in two rows, the first row is the node.
- A dependency id longer than 40 characters is an invalid id, and is not printed.
- The session ran without the plugin loaded: next-card and handoff were followed by hand, and the reviewer was a general agent given `agents/reviewer.md`. The transcript was passed to `card log-new` by hand. No pre-compact hook ran, so `compacted` is unknown.
